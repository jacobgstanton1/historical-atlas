# Historical Research Pipeline — Phase 3 Stage 1

This offline Node pipeline supports the atlas's 1800–1960 research programme. The live atlas remains v0.6.1. Research outputs are not production truth. Phase 2's 362/401 resolver availability remains a separate measure from field and temporal completeness.

The permanent layers are common date/fingerprint primitives; source catalogue; entity/year/category scanner; bounded job generator; specialist research packages; fail-closed validation and historical review; recoverable queue; serial append-only integrator; and separate machine/human reports. No module is imported by the browser.

Research is parallel; production integration is serial. Worker access restrictions are a protocol, not an OS sandbox: all Codex agents share filesystem access. Production fingerprints and immutability tests detect drift. Workers must write only their assigned package, not production, queue approvals or integration receipts. The coordinator owns queue transitions and reviews.

Exact dates use exclusive interval endpoints, matching the existing resolver. A year/month endpoint retains source precision and may overlap a transition year. Requested job periods are calendar periods; boundary snapshots never define historical fact dates. Observations are separate from validity intervals and must keep their observation date, metric and territorial scope. A nearby census is evidence context, not a census in the requested year.

The pipeline never infers sovereignty, succession, nationality or statistics from polygons, overlap, SUBJECTO/PARTOF, modern identities or another historical period. Missing evidence remains missing. Ambiguity routes to review; unresolved status is not silently promoted. Integration supports existing production fields only; Important Figures can be researched and validated outside production pending separately authorised consumer support.

Checkpoint plan: core schema/tooling; validator/tests; queue/reporting/documentation; controlled pilot. The pilot is limited to five existing entities and does not authorise global Deep Dossier research. Strong packages may remain accepted but unintegrated. Synthetic filesystem tests exercise the actual integrator without altering production.
