# Applicability accounting and limits

This is an offline audit of the current committed completion matrix and accepted production packages. It adds no historical facts, classifications or N/A resolutions. All 15 category questions are reviewed by explicit policy in research-applicability.mjs; the output preserves all 15,840 historical comparison slots.

Raw Evidence Coverage = supported / raw theoretical slots. Applicable Evidence Coverage = supported / (raw slots minus evidence-established N/A). Research Resolution = (supported plus independently evidence-established N/A, uncertainty, unavailability or whole-category territorial incompatibility) / raw assessment slots. Missing, partial and held states count in neither numerator. An incompatible candidate cannot resolve the whole category. Transition-year resolution must cover the entire selected snapshot.

No legitimate N/A, unavailable or uncertain resolution is currently recorded. Thus the denominator remains 15,840 and all three global metrics are 45.81%. This is a **provisional accountable denominator**, not a historical claim that all 15 concepts existed for every polity. Reducing it would require individual evidence-backed applicability assessments. Conditional fields, missing data, blocked density inputs and enrichment categories are not silently excluded.

The existing project's four-category CORE is identity, political framework, leadership and capital. There are 1,161 entirely missing core slots (594 leadership; 567 capital), plus 214 partial and 39 held core slots. Core evidence presence is 2,810/4,224 (66.52%). The separate extended core/reference view adds currency, population and area: 4,429/7,392 supported (59.92%), with 2,963 unresolved. These transparent profiles are management views, not changes to the canonical dossier or claims that other categories are unimportant.

Major Events and Important Figures are enrichment: useful dated contextual evidence, without a universal mandatory event/person quota. Their support remains 99/1,056 (9.38%) and 36/1,056 (3.41%). Their gaps remain in raw15 comparison but do not masquerade as missing core state facts. Density and Overview are derivation/synthesis workloads rather than automatic demands for new historical research. Conditional Flags, Relationships and Status require evidence-based applicability decisions rather than blanket assumptions.

“Supported” means the existing scanner finds qualifying evidence for the category, not that every conceivable subfield is documented. A sourced political-status fact does not prove complete constitutional information. A dated existing overview is not proof of exhaustive snapshot-specific narrative detail. Ordinary political-status records may support both Government/Politics and Context/Status; category slots are not independent unique facts. No semantic duplication is introduced by this audit. Unique accepted claims remain separately counted.

The registry records 7,474 missing slots without a category-specific source consultation in a temporally overlapping accepted package, and 285 missing slots with such a consultation. **This is not a verified count of wholly unresearched historical questions.** Legacy research, candidate-only packages and held material may lack a production investigation index. Therefore genuinely never-researched applicable slots cannot be established exactly from current bookkeeping. Consulted sources likewise do not prove exhaustive research or unavailable evidence. No uncertainty/unavailability conclusion is invented from these counts.

The regional ranking uses nontransition, entirely missing core slots first, then missing core slots per entity research group as an interval/source-reuse proxy. It does not pretend to measure safe future yield or credits. All 21 original cohorts are retained; overlapping dossiers are explicitly reported and regional totals must not be summed as a global denominator. The current top proxy is Batch 10, followed by Batch 17 and Batch 18. No regional campaign was begun.

Raw identity/snapshot review occurrences outside the resolved 1,056-dossier matrix remain separately visible. They cannot be assigned to modern states or multiplied into fictitious new category totals. The two pre-existing invalid-temporal scanner warnings remain excluded and explicitly reported; this audit neither repairs them silently nor counts them as usable facts.

Validation: 22 focused tests passed (nine new audit tests plus 13 existing completion tests), including fabricated N/A rejection, independently evidenced resolutions, transition bounds, partial/held handling, enrichment/core separation, overlapping cohorts, deterministic output and production immutability. No browser checks, source discovery, acquisition, held-case research or frontend/timeline changes occurred.
