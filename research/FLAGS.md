# Dated historical flag/symbol research

The `historical-flag` category uses the existing production `flags` arrays and existing requested-year renderer. A flag is a sourced historical fact, not a modern identity decoration. No modern fallback is permitted. Year/month endpoint uncertainty is preserved. Multiple dated assets may be visible during a transition calendar year.

Each string-valued claim adds `flag`: `asset`, `type`, `alt`, `license`, `attribution`, `assetSourceIds`, `sha256`, and `licenseUrl` for Creative Commons. Asset references must be local `./assets/flags/name.svg` paths; the hash binds exact bytes. Every asset provenance source must also appear in claim sources/evidence and resolve to a source with matching license/attribution and HTTPS `assetUrl`. Historical applicability and asset licensing are separate evidence questions; reading Commons alone does not establish every historical claim.

Accepted types: national flag, state flag, civil flag, state ensign, civil ensign, colonial ensign, royal standard, imperial standard, administrative flag, office standard, state symbol. Civil/colonial/office/royal designs remain explicitly qualified, not silently promoted to national flags. Competing historical representation requires independent review and can remain absent.

Workers stage assets outside production. The coordinator independently reviews the full historical/asset source and license before installing a reviewed asset. Never execute worker SVG or worker code. The validator rejects scripts, foreign objects, image embeddings, event handlers, external links/imports/resources, entity/DOCTYPE declarations, traversal, absent assets and changed hashes. Local fragment symbol references may be retained. If a technical XML declaration must be removed, preserve original staged bytes and both hashes, record the normalization, and do not change historical design. New assets are never AI generated or uncertain redraws.

Only after installation does deterministic validation pass; acceptance still requires an independent coordinator receipt binding package hash and review issues. Integration appends the existing production fields `asset`, `flagType`, `assetSha256`, `assetSourceIds`, `alt`, `license`, `licenseUrl`, `attribution`, temporal bounds and source IDs. Type is also included in the existing note rendered with citations. Existing records and assets remain untouched. The integrator never downloads, installs, replaces or deletes an asset.

New research-integrated assets contribute their hashes to production fingerprints, preserving all pre-Campaign-2 fingerprints. Integration journals retain reviewed asset hashes and refuse recovery if assets changed externally. `auditResearchFlags(context)` checks every newly integrated flag. This deterministic structural audit does not establish historical truth or licensing by itself.

Completeness reports include distinct entities, eligible entity/year pairs, fully supported, partial/qualified, historical-review and missing years. A flag lacking a dated start cannot fill lifetime coverage. Conservative endpoint coverage and distinct entity sets are reported separately; an entity can have supported years and missing years. Legacy flag records lacking explicit structured type remain qualified rather than being rewritten or assumed national. Resolver availability remains a separate metric.
