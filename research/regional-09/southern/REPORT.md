## Numeric economic observations: precise meaning

The university paper's Cuadro1gives public-administration GVA estimates in the monetary unit current during each historical year:1900=5,410,878;1914=16,378,647;1920=21,391,253;1945=89,610,880historicalUruguayanpesos. These are **sector estimates**, not GDP, public revenue, modern-currency figures or constant-price values.1920 is a budget-based benchmark; the other proposed years belong to the source's adjusted/interpolated historical series. All four claims explicitly preserve that qualification. The atlas performs no new interpolation or conversion. Full original source method must be independently reviewed before acceptance.
