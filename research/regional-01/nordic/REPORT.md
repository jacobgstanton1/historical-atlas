## Evidence and date boundaries

- Nationalbank historical coins gives rigsbankdaler introduced 1813 and unchanged currency name at 1818 transition; claim bounded specifically to 1815, rather than assuming later terminology.
- Riksdag constitutional history says the 1809 Instrument, with fundamental changes, was not replaced until 1974. The claim states framework continuity only; it does not assert unchanged franchise or royal authority.
- Norges Bank's chronological institutional history gives exact 19 March 1920 suspension and 8–22 September 1945 monetary reform. The latter uses month precision rather than inventing one event day.
- Althingi 2018 institutional guide page 8 states Supreme Court founded 1920; page 24 explicitly lists Thingvellir ceremonial meeting in 1930 for Althingi's millennium.
- Bohr's original Nobel biography gives Copenhagen professorship from 1916 and institute leadership 1920 until death 1962. Manchester years 1914–1916 excluded; wartime exile not projected as uninterrupted residence. Separate 1960 claim rests on institute leadership, not residence.
- Undset's Nobel autobiography gives Lillehammer residence since 1919 and postscript describes wartime exile and return. Candidate stops 1939; no automatic 1945 residence projection.
- Swedish Academy Lagerlof register gives Mårbacka residence 1909 until death, precise lifespan and dated literary works. Academy membership is qualified by 1914 election; not asserted in 1909.
- Aalto Foundation biographies establish Helsinki home completed 1936, foreign travel and American work, followed by renewed Finnish commissions and 1950s buildings. Candidate intervals deliberately separate prewar Helsinki association and postwar Finnish architectural activity; citizenship not inferred.
