Located one substantial table, **The World Almanac and Encyclopedia (1900), printed p367, “Statistics of the Countries of the World”**, in the digitised Boston Public Library copy. Original scan has explicit Country and Capitals columns. Source edition is contemporary reference evidence, not an official national gazette and not proof of each polity’s legal status.

`capital-table-manifest.json` preserves **100 nonblank literal capital cells**, with **44 suggestions referencing actual existing entity IDs**. All **100 remain held for historical review**. Every row has null `observedAt`, `validFrom` and `validUntil`: no publication-year interval was fabricated. `editionYear:1900` is bibliographic context only; `targetSnapshotCandidate:1900` is a future investigation target, not asserted applicability.

`transcribe-table.mjs` regenerates the manifest from the literal original-scan transcription and checks that suggested IDs actually exist. The source manifest records SHA-256 hashes for original metadata, full OCR, scan index and table facsimile. No OCR row joining was trusted blindly. Many dotted/blank capital cells were omitted; no names were filled from modern knowledge.

Critical date distinction: the p367 footnote **“Estimated for January 1, 1900” applies to the marked United States population cells**. It does not date every country row and does not establish dates for Capitals. Adjacent p368 and p369 ministry/head-of-government tables explicitly say December 1, 1899; that date cannot be transferred to p367. Capital observations may become production candidates only after a documented prefatory/table semantics review or other dated corroboration. Do not spend country-by-country resources forcing all 100 through.

Scope conflicts requiring preservation:

- British Empire, France and Colonies, United States and Colonies, Netherlands and Colonies, Portugal and Colonies, Italy and Colonies and Denmark and Colonies are source aggregate labels, not reviewed country mappings.
- Cambodia is literally assigned Saigon in the table. This likely conflates colonial administrative scope and needs review; no Cambodia/Indochina mapping suggested.
- Bolivia lists La Paz alone. Do not treat this as proof of sole or constitutional capital or discard Sucre’s distinction.
- German substate and several late-colonial labels have no reviewed matching curated entity at the table’s undated capital context. Their literal rows are preserved unmapped.
- Historic printed place spellings are retained; shortened/unclear forms such as Constantin’ple and S’ndershausen should be reconciled only with recorded evidence. The facsimile governs uncertain typography.
