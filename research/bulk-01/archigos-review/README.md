# Archigos academic tranche: independent review

`mapping-contracts.json` is a bounded historical mapping contract, not a production package. The source is **Tier B academic historical coding**, not an official institutional office register.

The reviewer read the original cached v4.1 codebook definition and coding rules (printed pages 1–5), and inspected relevant country-case context. The codebook distinguishes effective primary rulers from ceremonial office holders, documents some occupied administrations, and gives a case-specific methodology. Its dates and names must remain attributed to Archigos. Do not turn these into unqualified constitutional offices or claims of polygon-wide sovereignty.

`parser-certification.json` compares six core fields from a fresh direct pandas read of the original DTA against all 3,409 extracted records: 20,454 comparisons, no mismatch. The decoder's Latin1 fallback warning is preserved. The five accented historical candidates are held rather than silently repaired or name-expanded.

Only explicit contracts may be used. Their source country namespace and existing sourced historical entity bounds are both necessary; neither a present-day label nor polygon overlap is a substitute. Occupied/contested periods are excluded. Unlisted frameworks and countries remain held. A contract can truncate a spell at an existing curated framework boundary; this is a display boundary, not an asserted accession or departure date.

ENDDATE conventions cannot safely be generalized: United States transfers share a boundary date, while Swiss annual chairs end on December 31 before a January 1 successor. The permitted conservative adapter retains the original ENDDATE as an exclusive display cutoff, explicitly omitting the final coded day. It must not invent a next-day endpoint. Same-day/empty intervals are held. Switzerland and Uruguay's collegiate framework are held from this generic-primary-ruler tranche.

Explicit row holds include the Canada Borden narrative/table disagreement, the German imperial 1878 acting-regency note, and the East German record beginning before state formation. These are evidential holds, not defects to normalize away.

After normalization, independently compare each claim to its exact original DTA row, contract bounds, literal spelling, qualified role and source IDs. No birth/death, nationality, office title, cause of exit or post-tenure fact is approved by this tranche.
