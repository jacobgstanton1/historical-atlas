# Independent original-source review

The coordinator read the original CBN and Central Bank of The Gambia currency histories, Hassoum Ceesay's Bathurst history, the Nigeria Year Book 1966 and the Gambia colonial annual reports for 1900, 1914, 1920 and 1930. All 26 submitted claims are accepted with their existing qualifications.

Nigeria Year Book 1966 printed page 19 was visually checked: Clifford follows the 1919 entry; Bourdillon the 1935 entry; Richards the 1943 entry. Only interior year intervals are accepted; the ambiguous Thompson spelling and Lugard accession precision remain held. The publisher is Daily Times/Times Press, not the Nigerian government; the National Library preserves the volume.

Gambia 1920 printed page 2 was visually checked: revenue 268788 pounds belongs to 1920, not the neighbouring expenditure/assets columns. The 1900 revenue, 1914 exports including specie, and 1930 customs receipts retain their definitions and observation dates; none is GDP. Bathurst is a local administrative capital, not proof of an independent state. Currency circulation is not asserted to be exclusive. The late-1917 Gambian note introduction is preserved separately from earlier regional coinage.

Year-precision endpoints remain partial. Original source bodies are retained privately in the ignored cache; published hashes aid recovery without redistributing entire source publications. Nine held groups remain outside production.
