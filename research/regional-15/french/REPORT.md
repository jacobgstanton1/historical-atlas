# Batch 15 — French West African administrative systems

Worker packet only. No production registry, source registry, frontend, or Git checkpoint was modified by this worker. Coordinator review is required before any claim is accepted.

The reproducible builder emits 15 proposed claims using ten original institutional sources. Existing source IDs are reused by normalized URL. Royal Dahomey, French colonial Dahomey, AOF, occupied French Togo, mandated French Togo and independent Togo remain distinct.

## Source review and date discipline

- UNESCO's Abomey description expressly identifies the royal palaces as a political decision-making centre. Abomey is proposed only for the two royal entity windows, not French colonial Dahomey.
- Quai Branly's royal Glélé object catalogue identifies the1858–1889 reign. The proposed leadership interval uses only interior1878–1880 years. Dates attributed to other rulers were not adopted.
- Archives nationales authority record identifies Dakar as AOF capital. ANOM identifies the former Governor-General seat at Dakar. The post1904 research window deliberately avoids initial Saint-Louis headquarters. Independent reviewer should verify this continuity rather than treating federation creation1895 as proof Dakar was already its capital.
- René Tourte's CIRAD-hosted scholarly history explicitly dates Governor-General William Merlaud-Ponty1908–1915 (printed pp103/169). Only1909–1914 is proposed. Other governor transitions remain held.
- BCEAO's original monetary chronology explains issuance by BAO1901–1955, the AOF/Togo emission institute1955–1959 and BCEAO from1959. Its introduction dates colonial franc use to the nineteenth century. The Lomé agency appears in the1920–1924 chronology. The proposal distinguishes pre-CFA franc from the dated26December1945 CFA creation. Review must check geographical applicability; no modern-country currency assignment is used.
- BCEAO's parity table supports exactly1.70 French francs per CFA franc observed26December1945. This is an exchange parity, not GDP. The1960 parity observation is held because it precedes the independent entity window.
- The original French mandate reproduced by Université de Perpignan identifies France's administrative powers and obligations. The relationship does not assert annexation or include British Togoland. Source currently returns intermittent502 responses; exact document locator and previously registered source are retained for review.
- The U.S. Office of the Historian supports Togo independence27April1960. The event is separate from1950s trusteeship and the later1961 presidential framework.
- Assemblée nationale's Jean Jolly biography and mandate records provide Diagne's lifespan, Senegal association, election10May1914 and rights advocacy through1915–1916 legislation. Figure significance is based on the rights intervention, not officeholding alone. Colonial recruitment advocacy is preserved as a qualification; later1931 ministerial office is not back-projected.

## Held material and deliberate limits

CEPED/Groupe de Démographie Africaine Table90 supplies French Togo indigenous-population administrative estimates, including780,170 in1938 and912,425 in1945. Foreigners are excluded. These are preserved in held.json rather than presented as unqualified total polity populations. The1930 figure also conflicts with a scholarly citation of the contemporary annual report.

No density is derived from subgroup counts. No modern Benin or Togo population is used. Bank paid-in capital and colonial credit programmes are not relabelled GDP. Qualitative monetary context remains outside numeric-only Economy. Small municipal or banking decrees were not promoted to Major Events merely to fill a category.

No claimed gains are reported before independent review/integration. Interval reuse should make this bounded packet more useful than independent per-snapshot transcription. Research effort was capped on capital/leader exceptions; the packet is smaller than the aspirational25–50 claims because the inspected evidence did not justify that target.
