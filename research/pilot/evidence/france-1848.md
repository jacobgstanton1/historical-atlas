# France1848 political-institutional pilot

Research only; production and queue unchanged. Four dated claims preserve the intra-year change; no annual republican fallback.

- **pilot-france-july-monarchy** — [Monarchie de Juillet](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/histoire-et-patrimoine/monarchie-de-juillet). Read historical body sections on the revised Charter and February1848 monarchy (lines145–148,183–186). Supports constitutional monarchy under Louis-Philippe preceding revolution; no extrapolated ministry tenure or electoral counts.

- **pilot-france-february-revolution** — [Février1848 — Révolution de février](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/histoire-et-patrimoine/monarchie-de-juillet/revolution-de-fevrier). Read full historical body lines135–156: Louis-Philippe abdicates24February, attempted regency rejected, provisional government formed that evening and lower chamber dissolved; public republican proclamation described25February. Supplies monarchy end rather than geometry or later-state fallback.

- **pilot-france-provisional-republic** — [Février1848 — Gouvernement provisoire et proclamation de la Deuxième République](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/histoire-et-patrimoine/deuxieme-republique/gouvernement-provisoire-et-proclamation-de-la-deuxieme-republique). Distinguish revolutionary proclamation from constituent ratification; slavery chronology not used.

- **pilot-france-december-presidency** — [Décembre1848–mai1849 — Élection présidentielle et victoire de Louis-Napoléon Bonaparte](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/histoire-et-patrimoine/deuxieme-republique/election-presidentielle-et-victoire-de-louis-napoleon-bonaparte). ## Bounds and omissions

- No annual Republic label: monarchy until24February;25February–4May provisional framework; formal constituent proclamation4May; presidential institutional implementation only20December.
- July Monarchy and Republican legal claims are central institutions; this does not establish consent, uniform colonial administration, territorial borders or automatic successor identity.
- Exclusive interval endpoints include exact editorial year clips; these are partial research bounds rather than lifetimes. The provisional-government cutoff4May is a coverage decision, not the cabinet dismissal date.
- Source precision supports transition days; constitutional rules are distinguished from the timing of actual office entry. All claims require coordinator historical review.

- May–December detailed executive changes (Executive Commission and Cavaignac), ministries and transition hours are not proposed as government facts.
- November adoption/promulgation timing and retrospective seat counts are omitted because institutional summary pages are inconsistent.

## Source discrepancies excluded

- National Assembly constitution-adoption body lines142–143 describes adoption4November but promulgation12November and920 assembly members; its December body calls Constitution in force4November. The reproduced primary constitution has750 ordinary representatives and900 revising representatives. Neither disputed promulgation date nor misleading seat-count summary is in a claim.
- Public proclamation25February and formal constituent proclamation4May are distinct descriptions of revolutionary and juridical stages. No identical label is imposed across their different contexts.
- No flags, population, currency, economic facts, modern nationality, indigenous consent or inferred territorial succession are added.
