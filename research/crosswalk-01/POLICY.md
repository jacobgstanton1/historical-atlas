# Field-specific historical compatibility

Mappings bind an atlas entity, external dataset identifier/name, bounded interval, permitted fields, territorial comparison, rationale and evidence. A mapping is never permission to copy all fields of a modern country.

| Class | Meaning | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Exact historical entity/statistical unit | Automatic after deterministic validation and source review |
| B | Strong continuing historical equivalence despite naming/regime terminology | Independently reviewed, content-bound mapping; continuity established and no competing entity |
| C | Qualified quantitative scope proxy | Independently reviewed; population, area, density or economy only; dated observation and original scope retained |
| D | Partial, ambiguous or incompatible | Held |

Identity, government, leadership, capital, currency, flags and relationships require A/B. Statistical C permission never grants sovereignty or political equivalence. Important Figures and events retain their separate directly evidenced historical-association requirements. Overview requires accepted underlying evidence. Territorial classes distinguish EXACT, NEAR-EQUIVALENT, QUALIFIED-COMPATIBLE and INCOMPATIBLE; approximate evidence is never labelled exact. No numerical adjustment is inferred.

The validator checks field permissions, claim and requested-year bounds, source references, independent review hashes, continuity and competing-entity declarations. A/B/C review does not waive source quality, observation precision, conflict, licensing, entity-envelope or transition checks. Production audit rechecks accepted mappings. Density still requires compatible population and area inputs; a national statistical series is not automatically compatible with a mapped polygon.

Use `scripts/research-crosswalk.mjs` and `schemas/mapping.schema.json`. B/C review hashes bind all mapping content except the review itself. Preserve immutable original inputs and certify each candidate cohort through the existing completion integrator. Integration remains serial. Do not rerun candidate builders over an already-integrated cohort and overwrite its original certificate; create a new tranche instead.

`ft-intake` reruns the entire previous 46-observation hold set against cached original methodology and workbook cells. Independent review classifications are distinct from final coordinator dispositions: the proposed United States 1800 C proxy remains held because its reconstructed native-population geography is insufficiently established. Two reviewed B observations passed all remaining gates.

`capitals` uses the complete previously cached dated Wikidata capital extraction and existing framework evidence. ICOW is reconciliation context only; its restricted dataset is not republished. Generic capital statements do not establish exclusive, constitutional, wartime or temporary roles unless their evidence does so.

`residue-routing.json` inventories other preserved source families computationally. It is not an acceptance certificate or a claim that every residual received historical review. Review priority is expected newly supported slots divided by mapping effort. No name-only candidate flows into production.
