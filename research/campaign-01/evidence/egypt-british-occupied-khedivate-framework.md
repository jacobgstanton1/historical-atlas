# Campaign 1 evidence: egypt-british-occupied-khedivate-framework

Reused [Library of Congress country-study occupation chapter transcription](https://countrystudies.us/egypt/26.htm). Full body explicitly lists Cromer, Gorst and Kitchener with terms and identifies their British agent/consul-general role. This role is separate from the khedival government.

1883, 1907 and 1911 transition years intentionally remain gaps. Occupation is not converted into formal colonial annexation, British sovereignty or dynastic succession. Existing 18 December 1914 protectorate boundary is preserved.
