## Liberia, 1931–1960

Read complete FRUS 1951 despatch 722 (pp.1296–1300), saved as `liberia-frus.html`. Barclay's 1931 election, 1935 reelection and selection of Tubman for the 1943 election support the conservative 1932–1942 office interval. Do not adopt this colonial-era document's racial/electoral generalizations. The Liberia Senate's Nimba historical paragraph explicitly dates Tubman's administration 1944–1971; only 1945–1960 is proposed. Saved as `liberia-senate.html`.

CBL working paper 1/2016, printed pp.4–5, explicitly separates the 1913–1942 British West African pound period and 1943–1962 official US dollar period. Saved `liberia-currency.pdf`. Proposed interior intervals avoid the ambiguous transition years. This is currency research, not modern exchange-rate or statistical enrichment. Monrovia and a historical flag remain omitted: modern pages do not prove a selected historical interval.

## Belgian Congo, 1915–1938

Read Royal Museum for Central Africa publication footnote8 (PDF p.27), saved `congo-capital.pdf`: Boma preceded the 1923 transfer of the administrative centre to Léopoldville. Wider sources distinguish administrative transfer/implementation through1926, so the packages deliberately leave1923–1926 unfilled. Only Boma1915–1922 and Léopoldville1927–1938 are proposed. No governor tenure or colonial flag was fabricated. The current Congo central-bank history incorrectly calls Léopoldville the EIC capital; it was rejected as capital evidence rather than reused indiscriminately.

## Tonga, 1902–1959

Three interior intervals retain1921 and1936 as partial transition years. No post1967 pa’anga is projected backwards.

Queen Sālote's1919–1959 interval is supported by the complete contemporary article **A Kava Ceremony**, Te Ao Hou, November1964 p.6, read using the web tool: https://paperspast.natlib.govt.nz/periodicals/TAH196412.2.5.2 .
