# COLDAT v3 source-wide Relationship pass

The original Harvard Dataverse dataset, DOI 10.7910/DVN/T9SDEW, was acquired once with both tables, readme and methodology. Official version 3.0, updated 21 September 2023, is CC0 1.0. The complete dyad table contains 1,560 rows: 195 contemporary-country units × eight colonial powers; 161 positive colonial-history dyads. Zero-coded or absent records never resolve historical relationship absence.

The source explicitly offers two interpretations: last-date aggregation aims at event consensuality; rounded unweighted means aim at measurement accuracy and may fall between historical events. Neither is silently chosen as an exact constitutional transition date. Candidate intervals use the shared conservative interior of both interpretations, excluding boundary years. Original four date variables are retained verbatim. This is agreement between two aggregates, not proof that every underlying source agrees on every year.

Modern-country units can inherit colonial histories of absorbed territories. The v3 readme changes the area threshold from the methodology paper's older 5% to 10%. The modern unit is therefore not automatically the historical colony or polygon. Multiple colonial powers can refer to different territorial components or different episodes; first/last summary dates are not proof of continuous affiliation. Mandates, protectorates, occupations and personal unions are not collapsed into a more specific constitutional status.

Full-source exact-name/explicit-alias candidate join: 67 represented historical entities, 159 distinct entity/snapshot candidate slots, 148 already supported, ten unresolved held cases and one independently corroborated claim. Existing sourced primary names/status and full-year framework coverage are required; names alone never establish historical territory equivalence. The ten holds include Bhutan treaty interpretation, autonomous Suriname, post-1943 Lebanon, Kuwait treaty scope and Spanish-component coding of independent Morocco. Unmatched aliases remain unadjudicated; these figures are the conservative automatic join, not exhaustive historical equivalence adjudication. Do not investigate exceptions during this bulk phase.

The accepted claim copies the already-sourced literal **Paraguay — Spanish colonial province**, with its existing historical framework and explicit Spain counterpart. COLDAT corroborates rather than replaces that stronger source. Dataset version, modern source territory, colonizer, max/mean dates, interval clipping, mapping justification and original source IDs remain in the immutable claim/package. No modern-border or sovereignty inference was used.

**Actual metric outcome:** one unique Relationship claim integrated; Paraguay 1800 gains partial coverage, not a fully supported/resolved slot. Conservative year precision leaves the first boundary year partial under the unchanged scanner. Relationships: supported 467 → 467; partial 26 → 27; missing 562 → 561. No fabricated daily precision or scanner change was used to turn this into a full-slot gain. This is low yield; COLDAT was immediately followed by the HCD feasibility test.

Source: Becker, Bastian (2019), Introducing COLDAT: The Colonial Dates Dataset; COLDAT v3 (2023), https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/T9SDEW; provider https://www.beckerbastian.net/data. Local original files and byte-bound certification are preserved. No stronger accepted evidence was overwritten.
