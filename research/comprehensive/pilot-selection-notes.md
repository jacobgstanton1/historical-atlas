# Proposed bounded comprehensive pilot

Eight existing entities cover a European republic, empire, Asian republic, Latin American republic, African republic, colonial administration, small Himalayan monarchy and a constitutional transition. Periods are coherent and bounded; no external research was performed for selection. The machine-readable selection includes exact preserved package/evidence/review paths and production source IDs.

Campaign 2 is retired. Its strategic-stop handoff is authoritative over older readiness instructions. Existing preserved packages are reusable evidence, not automatic production truth. German, Brazilian and Liberian first-checkpoint material is already integrated; distinguish source reuse from newly discovered information. The other selected packages remain isolated.

Throughput limitations in the old architecture: fixed field/year scans encourage many small jobs and repeat discovery; four-field packages omit events, observations and contextual figures; full-package acceptance obscures mixed accepted/held claims; Important Figures and structured values currently lack production adapters; area observations are not safely represented by the old integrator; positive AD-only dates prevent future ancient chronology; serial fingerprint changes invalidate later acceptance receipts unless evidence review is separated from current-context deterministic acceptance. None of these weaknesses justify silently relaxing chronology or evidence review.

Recommended measurements: meaningful new claims per coherent assignment, sources consulted/reused/new, candidate acquisition versus accepted/review/rejected counts, applicable categories investigated, unresolved gaps, and separately production additions. Do not count a repeated sourced name or fragmented temporal span as new information. Record deterministic checks and actual browser smoke work independently. Stop and reassess if the pilot is only repackaging a few old facts rather than increasing useful information per source pass.

Before research: finalize/test comprehensive schema, candidate import/provenance, per-category applicability, mixed claim disposition, selected-year and observation safeguards, structured figure validation and serial recovery. Source-body review remains necessary for historical interpretation; deterministic checks should handle references, dates, overlaps and duplication. Do not repeat global browser regression for data-only work.
