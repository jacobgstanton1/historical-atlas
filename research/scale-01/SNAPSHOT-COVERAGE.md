# Snapshot coverage protocol

The primary research denominator is snapshot × represented historical entity × applicable category. `scripts/research-snapshot-scan.mjs` reads the current `SNAPSHOTS` block in `app.js` without executing frontend code. Manifest raw-identity presence supplies the geometry snapshot; dated registry mappings independently determine historical identity. Community/people identities and Antarctica are excluded. Aliases resolving to one entity are deduplicated. Competing mappings, broken references and unresolved classifications retain explicit review cases. No geometry, SUBJECTO or PARTOF establishes political identity.

Call `scanSnapshots(readContext())`, or supply explicit `snapshots`, `entityIds`, and an accepted rich store for controlled analysis. Future BCE configurations use astronomical years and the existing date-aware chronology; no future global schedule is encoded. A snapshot year is currently a calendar-year context, consistent with the resolver. An intra-year transition is partial or historical review, rather than a silently chosen January1 world state.

All14 comprehensive categories are reported independently using sourced legacy and accepted production-rich records. Missing provenance, unresolved evidence, contradictory same-role leadership, territorial scope mismatch, imprecise temporal boundaries and invalid licensed flag assets fail closed or require review. Sourced explicit non-applicability is counted separately. Core coverage requires identity, political institutions, leadership and capital; substantially complete means those core categories plus at least10supported categories. These operational labels measure research breadth, not historical certainty or proof that every role or institution is exhaustively documented.

A dated fact is stored once. Reports distinguish unique dated records, unique supporting records, supported category slots, claim-to-snapshot applications and additional reuse applications. Equivalent rich and legacy representations are deduplicated. Existing362/401resolver availability remains a separate metric. Annual integrity APIs are retained; annual missing opportunities are not the headline.

Past observations and events can provide nearby snapshot context under independently configurable five-year windows. This is a relevance heuristic, not interpolation: actual observation/event dates, geographic scope, sources and qualifications remain in every report record. Future dates never provide retrospective coverage. Rich statistics, nearby observations and dated-event context require explicit compatible scope. Exact-snapshot legacy observations may retain their sourced, qualified geography as recorded; a legacy scope string alone cannot authorize carry-forward into a nearby snapshot. Undated evidence cannot silently become permanent coverage. An explicit sourced date-aware investigation may mark a category not applicable; those slots are excluded from the applicable denominator.

`writeSnapshotReport(report, outputDirectory)` produces deterministic JSON and Markdown. The standalone CLI accepts an optional output directory. `result.gaps` supplies the existing bounded job-generator interface, and `result.productionFingerprint` binds its context. An optional queue array or queue.jobs array supplies state totals. Scanning does not mutate input, production files, registries or map behavior. Tests use small deterministic fixtures for mappings, source failures, deduplication, BCE, observation handling, context windows, transitions, category applicability and reuse. No frontend change is involved, so browser checks are unnecessary.

Limits: the manifest is an audited presence inventory and must be regenerated when geometry inputs change. Year-only boundaries preserve ambiguity. A five-year nearby-evidence window is not a substitute for historical review; it may be reduced or disabled. Core coverage does not assert exhaustive head-of-state/head-of-government roles. Formal legal frameworks can differ from actual practice. Resolver availability and snapshot research completeness remain distinct measures.

The principal CLIs now default to this model: `node scripts/research-scan.mjs`, `node scripts/research-report.mjs`, and `node scripts/research-generate.mjs --limit 3 --entities <existing IDs>`. Use `--annual-integrity` explicitly for the retained annual tooling. Programmatic `scan` and `generateJobs` remain available for earlier integrity fixtures. `generateSnapshotJobs` combines several category gaps and real target snapshots into one bounded comprehensive assignment, retains all14 investigation categories, blocks duplicate active work including pre-amendment jobs, and never turns unresolved raw identities into guessed entities.

The authoritative persisted headline report is `research/reports/research-report.json` and its Markdown companion. Its `deduplicated-snapshot-report` format indexes evidence in `records` by claim ID. Snapshot/category slots reference those records and retain context mode; absent empty record/issue arrays mean no evidence/issues. Compact gaps carry entity, snapshot, category, priority and any specific review reason; full job-generation context remains available from the read-only scanner API. This avoids repeating source bodies, facts and mapping definitions in thousands of missing slots. Generated jobs are bounded proposals, not automatically accepted or integrated work.

The two legacy undated name entries (United States and Soviet Union) are excluded from dated-claim metrics and listed in report errors. Existing dated identity records remain usable; this diagnostic does not authorize rewriting those registries. Non-applicability must cover the full snapshot-year context, and Important Figure relevance must remain within recorded lifespan boundaries.
