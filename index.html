About Historical Atlas

Explore political territories and their historical dossiers across selected world-map snapshots from 4000 BCE to 1960.

Selected year
The historical year you are exploring. Dossier facts are shown in that year's context.
Boundary snapshot
The map uses the nearest available snapshot. Its date is shown separately when it differs from your selected year.
Fact applicability
Names, offices and other facts have their own dated periods. Transition-year entries retain those qualifications.
Observation date
A census or estimate may have a different date. Statistics retain that date and the territory they measure.

Historical borders and political status can be uncertain or disputed. Map geometry alone does not establish sovereignty, and statistical territories may differ from mapped boundaries.

Substantive dossier facts retain citations. Open Sources & Methodology in an entry to inspect its references, dates and qualifications.

Boundary maps: Historical Basemaps. The atlas presents selected snapshots, not a continuous reconstruction of every border change.
