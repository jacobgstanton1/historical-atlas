## Decisions and limitations

- All dossiers remain partial; bounds indicate researched coverage, not complete state lifetimes. No unsupported currency, statistics, flags, seats or succession links.
- Except Württemberg, assigned 1800 phases remain gaps pending earlier institutional and office research. Narrow 1815 coverage does not bridge Napoleonic annexation or occupation.
- Mecklenburg’s 1815 elevation retains year precision; the shared Estates did not unify the states. Strelitz’s Ratzeburg and domains were outside representation.
- Oldenburg distinguishes formal Duke Peter Friedrich Wilhelm from Administrator Peter Friedrich Ludwig, who did not adopt the granted grand-ducal title.
- Lippe’s unsuccessful 1819 constitutional proposal is outside coverage; Schaumburg-Lippe’s 1816 constitution is a real framework change.
- Waldeck and Pyrmont remained in personal union in this interval. Neither modern dependency nor later real union is inferred.
- Saxony’s restored profile starts at the documented 7 June 1815 return. Earlier occupation is uncurated; king and elector use different regnal numbers.
- Nassau’s 1814 Estates constitution does not establish a sitting legislature in 1815. Hesse profiles do not project later titles or constitutions backward.
- Yugoslavia retains provisional, constitutional, royal-authoritarian and postwar frameworks. 1921/1929/1931/1945/1946/1953 transitions remain visible; 1939–early 1945 is a gap. Detailed regency after 1934 and named leadership in the 1953 election year remain incomplete.
- Danzig is neither a generic German state nor an unrestricted sovereign republic. Later Nazi administration is uncurated.
- Source retrieval intermittently failed for individual compendium articles after earlier successful body reads. Scholarly descriptions, not geometry or unsourced snippets, support accepted facts.

Lippe and Schaumburg-Lippe, both Mecklenburg states, both Hessian states, and Schleswig/Holstein remain deliberately distinct. Yugoslav constitutional/regime frameworks remain dated; no geometry-derived succession chain or modern fallback. Württemberg’s electoral and royal offices use separate titles and regnal styles. Existing reference and earlier batch records remain immutable under checkpoint tests.
