## Identity decisions and gaps

- All accepted dossiers are partial. Curated bounds are not complete state lifetimes. Missing currency, statistics, flags, offices, seats and predecessor/successor links remain omitted rather than inferred.
- The unified Two Sicilies kingdom dates from 1816. The assigned 1800/1815 raw label is not accepted at either snapshot; sourced 1817–1819 between-snapshot coverage does not count as snapshot political coverage.
- Anhalt is not resolved to one of the separate Bernburg, Dessau or Köthen states. The source referent requires geometry/source-family review.
- Hohenzollern is not resolved arbitrarily to Hechingen or Sigmaringen. Independent archives distinguish the principalities.
- Italy 1922–1929 and July 1943–May 1945 remain gaps; competing wartime administrations are not collapsed into a single royal government. June 1946 royal transfer retains month precision.
- Czechoslovakia 1918–1920, 1938–October 1945 and 1946–1948 remain gaps; constitutional adoption is not substituted for unverified commencement in 1920.
- Lucca 1815 was administered provisionally by Austria; Maria Luisa’s actual government begins in 1817 at source year precision.
- Restored Italian duchies are not projected into 1800. Lombardy and Venetia are administrative governments, not sovereign states.
- Baden and Hanover retain especially narrow 1815 institutional coverage. No population/GDP/flag enrichment was attempted.

Italy and Kingdom of Italy consolidate only within the documented unified liberal monarchy. Later Fascist, postwar royal, provisional republican and 1948 constitutional frameworks remain distinct dated records. Czechoslovakia uses separate early and postwar frameworks, then one Communist constitutional profile with the dated 1960 change. Bavaria’s electoral and royal offices remain distinct. Lucca’s Austrian and Bourbon administrations remain separate. No polygon-derived relationships or succession chains.
