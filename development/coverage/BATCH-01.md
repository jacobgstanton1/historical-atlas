## Architecture and limitations

Retain the two static JSON files and cached temporal resolver. No backend, production split, geometry change or UI redesign. A data cache query increment loads the expansion while visible v0.6.1 remains.

Finland 1917, Iceland 1918 and Switzerland 1848 deliberately report calendar-year identity ambiguity. Other intra-year changes preserve independently sourced dates and can show multiple records. Snapshot absence is not dissolution. Curated bounds may delimit verified coverage rather than legal creation/abolition; record notes explain this. Formal monarchs and collective institutions retain their actual offices; unresearched practical leadership remains omitted.

Population and flag enrichment is deferred pending comparable scope or historical applicability/licensing evidence. Early currencies, wartime seats, interim officeholders and constitutional gaps remain documented above. Austrian Netherlands 1800 and Luxembourg 1800 are not given anachronistic mappings; Portugal 1800 remains uncurated. Legacy heuristic inventory warnings remain traceable separately from reviewed historical decisions. No Batch 02 work.
