## Source evidence

- b11-puerto-rico-crs: [Puerto Rico: A Chronology of Political Status History, 97-526, 1998](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/97-526.html) — Library of Congress, Congressional Research Service (public report mirror); Body read: Introduction and chronology: 1900 Foraker civil government; Jones Act 2 March 1917 elected House/Senate; 1947 elective-governor legislation; 1952 proclamation and republican constitution. 1948 first-governor service and July 1950 date inconsistencies not used for precise transition dates.
- b11-swedish-saint-barthelemy: [Swedish Caribbean Colonialism: About the project](https://swecarcol.ub.uu.se/info/about?lang=en) — Uppsala University Library; Body read: Project body identifies Swedish colonial Saint-Barthélemy during 1784–1878 and later transfer to France; archive scope, not a modern sovereign-state fallback.
- b11-seners-islands: [Saint-Martin, Saint-Barthélemy: quel avenir pour les îles du nord de la Guadeloupe?, 1999](https://www.vie-publique.fr/files/rapport/pdf/004000186.pdf) — French Ministry responsible for Overseas Territories, François Seners; Body read: PDF pages 5–8: French/Dutch partition; Swedish administration and French attachment 16 March 1878; practical near-absence of colonial administration 1880–postwar. French-side Saint-Martin cannot be assigned to an ambiguous whole-island raw identity.
- b11-saint-kitts-union: [Saint Christopher and Nevis, 5 May 1983, HC Deb vol 42](https://api.parliament.uk/historic-hansard/commons/1983/may/05/saint-christopher-and-nevis) — UK Parliament, Hansard; Body read: Minister’s historical opening, columns 515–516: Saint Christopher and Nevis united with Anguilla by federal Act 1882; associated statehood only 1967. No 1882 commencement-day inference.
- b11-saint-lucia-constitution: [Constitutional Development](https://www.govt.lc/constitutionaldevelopment) — Government of Saint Lucia; Body read: Historical body 1808–1951: Crown-colony Orders-in-Council, 1816/1832 councils, Windward administrator, 1924 letters patent/1925 elected council, reserve powers 29 October 1936, adult-suffrage framework 1951. Later modern independence text not projected.

- b11-virgin-islands-doi: [U.S. Virgin Islands: History and Political Status](https://www.doi.gov/oia/islands/virgin-islands) — US Department of the Interior, Office of Insular Affairs; Body read: History body: prior Danish islands, US purchase 1917, citizenship 1927, Interior authority 1931, Organic Act 1936 and revised Act 1954. Modern leadership section excluded.
- b11-trinidad-parliament: [Evolution of a Nation: Trinidad and Tobago at Fifty, 2012](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Evolution-of-a-Nation-Trinidad-and-Tobago-at-50.pdf) — Parliament of Trinidad and Tobago; Body read: PDF pages 27–31 and 71–77: early Council of Advice; Governor and nominated councils before 1925; elected minority from 1925; 1945 franchise and 1950 reforms. Counts vary and are not generalized across decades.
- b11-trinidad-union-order: [Trinidad and Tobago Order in Council 1888](https://laws.gov.tt/pdf/A2.pdf) — Trinidad and Tobago Ministry of Legal Affairs; Body read: Two-page original historical Order, clauses 1–3: united colony effective 1 January 1889; Tobago councils ended; Governor instructions extended. 2012 compilation date not treated as a contemporary rule.
- b11-bahamas-constitutional-report: [Report of the Constitutional Commission, July 2013](https://cdn.bahamas.gov.bs/tenant/tenanttgotb/documents/All%20Documents/ConstitutionCommissionReport20138JULY2013-20240119174548.pdf) — Government of The Bahamas, Constitutional Commission; Body read: Section 5, PDF pages 47–50: Royal Governor, elected Assembly, nominated Council; 1909 separation of executive and legislative councils; internal self-government constitution only 1964. No modern Senate before 1964.
