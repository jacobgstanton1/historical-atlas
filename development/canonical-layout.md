# Canonical political-entity dossier presentation

Every classified political entity/dependent administration, including dossiers without resolver data, uses Identity Header; Overview; Government & Politics; Leadership; Population & Territory; Economy; Major Events; Important Figures; Historical Relationships; Historical Context & Status; Sources & Methodology. Existing community/people classifications are projected from the committed coverage manifest into dossier-layout.js; they are not reclassified and do not receive state-oriented fields.

Header positions remain stable. Missing header values use Unavailable; empty sections use one restrained missing-data state. Uncertain requires explicit existing uncertainty; Not applicable requires an explicit underlying declaration and is never inferred from absence. Methodology is collapsed last, with boundary context nested inside it. Separate transition frameworks keep named/date-qualified groups within the fixed section order.

Exchange quotations remain untouched in production. Presentation groups counterpart/unit/method records, selects an exact-selected-year observation (latest within that year) or nearest earlier applicable observation from the unchanged resolver, and displays its actual date and citation. Remaining quotations are chronologically inspectable behind Additional historical quotations (N), with full source scope/qualifiers available through Quotation scope & notes. No economic series is interpolated or retimed.
